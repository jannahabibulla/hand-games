Air Hero

Play the melody in the air. Turn on the camera, pick a song, and press Start. Notes fall down the lanes: have your index finger over a circle when its note lands and you play that note. Hold a finger on one circle for repeated notes, or move between circles as the tune moves. Both hands work. No camera? Use A S D F G or click the lanes.

Works best in Chrome. Your camera picture stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
