Cross the Road

Get the chicken as far up as you can. Turn on the camera and hold up one hand. Swipe your hand up, down, left, or right to aim the arrow, then pinch (thumb to index finger) to hop that way. A dashed square shows where you'll land. Dodge cars, ride logs across rivers, go around trees. No camera? Drag to aim and click to hop, or use the arrow keys and Space.

Works best in Chrome. Your camera picture stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
